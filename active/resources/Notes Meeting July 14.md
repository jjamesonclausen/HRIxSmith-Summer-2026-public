

The primary objective is to validate an AI-augmented design process, not to create a superior vertical axis wind turbine.

- Successful design of the turbine using the AI approach serves as validation for the process's worth.
- Key outcomes are insights, lessons learned, and the process itself, rather than the final design.
- Documentation of CFD runs, CAD prompts, and results is ongoing.
- Learning and Proficiency with Agentic CAD:
	- An open question exists on the optimal approach to learning agentic CAD tools.
	- Consideration: Start with simpler designs (e.g., teacup) before complex ones, or directly attempt the complex design.
-  The current project is seen as an initial experiment, with future iterations informed by these learnings.
- The team has largely skipped simpler designs and proceeded directly to the turbine design.
- Learning ZooKeeper CAD has focused on the KCL (code-based) approach, perceived as more intuitive than a point-and-click interface by some.
- A potential future approach: use agentic CAD for the initial 80% of the design and then a traditional CAD tool for the final 20% cleanup.
- This approach might be beneficial due to potential struggles in cleaning up complex designs with agentic tools.
- One team member prefers the KCL approach for its intuitiveness over button-heavy interfaces, though it requires learning a coding language.
- Another team member has focused on using AI prompts to learn the point-and-click interface and identify lines of code for edits, finding this more effective at times.
