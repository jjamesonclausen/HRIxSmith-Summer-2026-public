## Figure Selection Notes

- The VA20 figure is a geometry and computational-domain drawing, not a photograph; it is the source figure that directly shows the involute rotor and its wind-flow modifier. (source: `sources/va20.md`, Fig. 6)
- The VA23 and VJ9 figures compare multiple designs. Their captions identify the 50% shifted-troposkien and scooplet-based configurations respectively, so a final report should crop or annotate the selected subfigure if visual clarity is needed. (source: `sources/va23.md`, Fig. 1; source: `sources/vj9.md`, Fig. 16)
- These figures document source designs and concepts only. They do not establish that a concept is suitable for the Boston Logan application or that it was selected for later CAD work. (source: `active/analysis/Concept ranking worksheet.md`)
