## Three-Turbine Savonius Cluster

The source studies a three-turbine cluster built from a conventional two-blade Savonius rotor with overlap ratio `0.1`, `D = 0.95 m`, and `H = 1 m`. (source: sources/vj13.md)

- T1, T2, and T3 are arranged as a compact cluster and evaluated under eight wind directions and four wind speeds. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The source focuses on how installation orientation changes the annual output of this fixed geometry rather than on changing the rotor shape itself. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- Reported annual output ratios are about `1.25`, `1.27`, and `1.16/1.17` relative to an isolated turbine. (source: sources/vj13.md)
