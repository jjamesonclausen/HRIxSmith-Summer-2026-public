## Double Darrieus with Inner H-rotor and Outer Eggbeater

A hybrid VAWT configuration that nests a Darrieus rotor inside another Darrieus rotor arrangement. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - The concept combines an inner H-rotor with an outer eggbeater-style Darrieus. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Performance:
  - The report lists it as a candidate hybrid configuration explored in the broader design space. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
