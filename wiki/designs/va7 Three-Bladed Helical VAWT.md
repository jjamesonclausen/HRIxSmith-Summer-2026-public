## Three-Bladed Helical VAWT

The `va7` study analyzes a three-bladed helical VAWT generated by helically extruding a NACA0015 airfoil with no pitch and then varying the helix angle. (source: sources/va7.md)

## Geometry

- The blade geometry is generated from a NACA0015 airfoil with no pitch. (source: sources/va7.md)
- The blade chord is 210 mm. (source: sources/va7.md)
- Turbine diameter is 1 m and turbine height is 3 m. (source: sources/va7.md)
- The study compares helical sweep angles of 60 degrees, 90 degrees, and 120 degrees. (source: sources/va7.md)
- A 200 mm diameter shaft is included in the modeled geometry. (source: sources/va7.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The source studies helical blades as a way to spread blade loading over a wider azimuth range and reduce shaft ripple. (source: sources/va7.md)

## Performance

- The 60 degree helical blade gives the maximum reported power performance in the source. (source: sources/va7.md)
- Larger helix angles reduce the standard deviation of `Cp`, making the loading smoother. (source: sources/va7.md)
