## Counter-rotating Dual-Rotor VAWT

This `vj12` design is a stacked dual-rotor counter-rotating VAWT in which two rotors are mounted on the same vertical axis and rotate in opposite directions. (source: sources/vj12.md)

## Geometry

- The design places one rotor above the other so both receive wind directly from the site rather than using the wake of the first rotor. (source: sources/vj12.md)
- The two rotors share the same vertical axis and use a single generator arrangement. (source: sources/vj12.md)
- The source says only one rotor is mounted on the shaft, while the other is mounted on the magnetic coil of the generator to create relative rotational speed. (source: sources/vj12.md)
- Each rotor is fixed to its rotation direction with unidirectional bearings. (source: sources/vj12.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- Opposite rotation is mandatory in this layout to avoid zero net output from cancellation if both rotors spin the same way. (source: sources/vj12.md)
- The source says the arrangement can keep producing power even if only one rotor is spinning. (source: sources/vj12.md)
- A later spacing study cited in the review found that smaller top-to-bottom rotor spacing performed better, with an optimum around 10 cm. (source: sources/vj12.md)

## Performance

- The review says the first counter-rotating Darrieus design nearly doubled output relative to a single-rotor turbine with the same design. (source: sources/vj12.md)
- The same source says that design had a relatively high starting speed of 5 m/s. (source: sources/vj12.md)
