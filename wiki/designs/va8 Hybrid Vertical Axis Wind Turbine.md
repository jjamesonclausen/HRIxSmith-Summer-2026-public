## Hybrid Vertical Axis Wind Turbine

The `va8` patent discloses a hybrid vertical-axis wind turbine that combines lift-generating asymmetrical blades with drag-generating compartmented horizontal channel beams. (source: sources/va8.md)

## Geometry

- The turbine has a central vertical rotating shaft with upper and lower hubs. (source: sources/va8.md)
- Each blade is connected to upper and lower horizontal channel beams. (source: sources/va8.md)
- The channel beams include compartments that act similarly to a Savonius device and generate drag torque. (source: sources/va8.md)
- The blade profile is asymmetrical with unequal upper and lower cambers. (source: sources/va8.md)
- Power generation and storage hardware are placed at the bottom of the turbine. (source: sources/va8.md)

![Source figure](../../images/va8-fig1.jpg)
Original caption: Figure 1: External perspective view of the vertical axis wind turbine. [[va8|Source]]

## Unique Design Choices

- The source presents the design as a hybrid Savonius-Darrieus concept in which lift from the blades is complemented by drag torque from the compartmented beams. (source: sources/va8.md)
- The patent emphasizes bottom-mounted generation/storage hardware as a stability improvement over top-mounted arrangements. (source: sources/va8.md)

## Performance

- The patent claims the blade profile can generate high starting torque even at low wind. (source: sources/va8.md)
- It also claims wind-tunnel testing showed similar torque magnitude when wind comes from the leading or trailing edge. (source: sources/va8.md)
- In the patent's worked example, the present profile generated 2.5 times the net circumferential force of a typical flat-bottom profile. (source: sources/va8.md)
