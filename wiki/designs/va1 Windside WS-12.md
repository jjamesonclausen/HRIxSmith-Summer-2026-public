## Windside WS-12

The WS-12 is a drag-driven VAWT chosen in `va1` as one of the two candidate urban turbines for rooftop evaluation in Trieste. (source: sources/va1.md)

## Geometry

- Height: 6 m. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Diameter: 2 m. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Swept area: 12 m2. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Blade material: aluminum. (source: sources/va1.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The source describes WS-12 as a custom-made unit for specific applications. (source: sources/va1.md)
- It is characterized as an evolution of the Savonius-type turbine aimed at taking advantage of low wind speeds. (source: sources/va1.md)
- The source says it does not need to be stopped during storms like propeller-type turbines. (source: sources/va1.md)

## Performance

- Rated power: 3.9 kW. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Cut-off wind speed: none. (source: sources/va1.md)
- The source presents WS-12 as the lower-`Pnom/A` but more low-wind-oriented candidate relative to QR5. (source: sources/va1.md)
