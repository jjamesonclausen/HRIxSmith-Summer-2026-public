## Modified Classical Savonius

The `vj9` source's first optimized design is a modified classical Savonius rotor that remains close to the conventional shape while adjusting simple circular-arc geometry. (source: sources/vj9.md)

## Geometry

- The source says the first optimization run converged almost to a classical Savonius with overlap ratio `s/d = 0.1`. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- In the optimized geometry, the blade radius of curvature is 193 mm and the blade spans an angle of 167 degrees. (source: sources/vj9.md)

Original caption: Figure 16: Optimization results: a) modified classical Savonius b) the scooplet-based design c) separated scoop from the scooplet-based design. [[vj9|Source]]

## Unique Design Choices

- The design intentionally keeps a simple constant-thickness circular-segment construction rather than moving to more complex spline or elliptical shapes. (source: sources/vj9.md)

## Performance

- The source reports that the slight shape modification improved power coefficient by 4.5% relative to the classical reference. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The paper says the modified classical design reaches performance comparable to Bach-type optimized designs while remaining simple to manufacture. (source: sources/vj9.md)
