## Ice-Type VAWT

The `va13` study models an IceWind-type rooftop VAWT as its second building-retrofit case. (source: sources/va13.md)

## Geometry

- Rotor diameter is 1.515 m. (source: sources/va13.md)
- The case is described as a three IceWind blade turbine intended for compact urban application. (source: sources/va13.md)

![Source figure](../../images/va13-fig6.jpg)
Original caption: Figure 6. Turbine designs for each case and rooftop installation (developed by authors). [[va13|Source]]

## Unique Design Choices

- The source frames the IceWind case as the more compact urban-friendly alternative to the helical case. (source: sources/va13.md)

## Performance

- Reported rated power output is 430 W. (source: sources/va13.md)
- Reported maximum power coefficient is 0.17. (source: sources/va13.md)
- On the modeled building, this case reduced annual energy use by 22.93% and had a reported payback period of 10.60 years. (source: sources/va13.md)
