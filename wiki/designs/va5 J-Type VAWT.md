## J-Type VAWT

A small drag-based vertical-axis wind turbine design using J-shaped blades. (source: sources/va5.md)

- Geometry:
  - Three J-shaped drag blades are mounted on a 1 m rotor diameter and paired with a DC generator. (source: sources/va5.md)
  - The design targets rooftops and small domestic loads. (source: sources/va5.md)
- Performance:
  - Target rated power is 35 W with a cut-in speed of 3 m/s and a rated speed of 6.67 m/s. (source: sources/va5.md)
  - Reported prototype efficiency is 23.3%. (source: sources/va5.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - It is compact, quiet, and easy to install and maintain, but the paper notes manufacturing errors and friction reduced performance. (source: sources/va5.md)

- The paper uses the design for rooftop deployment and low-cost rural electrification. (source: sources/va5.md)
- The rotor is three-bladed and paired with a direct-drive generator. (source: sources/va5.md)
- The target design point is 35 W at 6.67 m/s. (source: sources/va5.md)
- The reported prototype efficiency is 23.3%. (source: sources/va5.md)
