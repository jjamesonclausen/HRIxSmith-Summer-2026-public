## AeroVironment AVX1000

The `va19` campus study evaluates the `AeroVironment AVX1000` as a rooftop-edge wind turbine specifically intended for parapet installation in urban flow. (source: sources/va19.md)

## Geometry

- The source describes it as an upwind turbine with `5 ft 6 in` diameter and maximum power production of `1 kW`. (source: sources/va19.md)
- The report gives a swept area of `2.21 m^2`. (source: sources/va19.md)
- It says the turbine has only `60 degrees` of turning capability and is intended to be installed in systems of six or more along a building roofline. (source: sources/va19.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The source says AV developed the machine specifically to use the updraft and shear-zone speed-up about `2 to 3 ft` above the top edge of a building. (source: sources/va19.md)
- According to the same source, that parapet effect could produce `15% to 40%` more power. (source: sources/va19.md)

## Performance

- The report says the AV system can look efficient on a swept-area basis at higher wind speeds, but not when turbine and installation costs are included. (source: sources/va19.md)
- In the report's economic analysis, no AVX1000 campus scenario was close to feasible and even the best case had roughly `90 years` payback. (source: sources/va19.md)
- The side-by-side comparison table in the source gives a lifetime electricity cost of about `$0.67/kWh` for the Eastgate `6 kW` AV system. (source: sources/va19.md)
