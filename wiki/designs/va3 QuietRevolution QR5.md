## QuietRevolution QR5

The QR5 is a helical urban wind turbine designed for lower wind speeds and frequently changing wind directions. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Geometry

- Physical dimensions: 5 m high x 3.1 m diameter. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rotor construction: carbon fiber and epoxy resin blades and connection arms. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Generator: direct-drive, mechanically integrated, weather-sealed 6 kW permanent-magnet generator. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Minimum recommended roof-mounting height above buildings: 3 m. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Minimum tower mast height: 9 m to bottom of blades. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The helical blade design is described as minimizing vibration and reducing acoustic noise. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Performance

- Rated power: 6 kW. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Expected output: 9,600 kW.hr/year at 5.9 m/s average annual wind speed. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The turbine self-maintains in steady wind of 4.0-4.5 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Operation mode lists maximum wind speed as 16 m/s and minimum wind speed as 4 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Overspeed braking begins above 14 m/s and automatic shutdown occurs above 16 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Design lifetime: 25 years. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The source says QR5 uses a predictive controller that learns site wind conditions and actively spins up the turbine when sufficient wind exists. (source: sources/va3.md)
