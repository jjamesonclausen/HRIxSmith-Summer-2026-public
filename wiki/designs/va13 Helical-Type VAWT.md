## Helical-Type VAWT

The `va13` building-retrofit study models a three-bladed helical rooftop VAWT as one of its three comparison cases. (source: sources/va13.md)

## Geometry

- The design uses three blades. (source: sources/va13.md)
- Rotor diameter is 1.515 m. (source: sources/va13.md)

![Source figure](../../images/va13-fig6.jpg)
Original caption: Figure 6. Turbine designs for each case and rooftop installation (developed by authors). [[va13|Source]]

## Unique Design Choices

- The source selects the helical form as a moderate-wind urban rooftop option and compares it directly against IceWind and a combined rotor. (source: sources/va13.md)

## Performance

- Reported rated power output is 350 W. (source: sources/va13.md)
- Reported maximum power coefficient is 0.21. (source: sources/va13.md)
- On the modeled building, this case reduced annual energy use by 18.45% and had a reported payback period of 12.89 years. (source: sources/va13.md)
