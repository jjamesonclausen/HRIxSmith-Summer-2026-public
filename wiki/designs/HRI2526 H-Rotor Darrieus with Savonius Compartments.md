## H-Rotor Darrieus with Savonius Compartments

This HRI prototype combines an H-rotor Darrieus layout with Savonius compartments and was one of the four concepts selected for initial prototyping and wind-tunnel testing. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

## Geometry

- The report names this concept as `H-Rotor Darrieus With Savonius Compartments` in the four-prototype set. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Like the other HRI prototypes, it was modeled in CAD and 3D printed at 5 inches tall by 4.5 inches wide for comparative wind-tunnel testing. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The design represented a different low-wind-startup strategy from the Classical Savonius, EN0005, and Helical Hybrid concepts. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

## Performance

- During wind-tunnel testing, this design was unable to cut in. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Because it could not cut in and lacked enough supporting literature for further optimization within the project timeline, the team removed it from further development. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
