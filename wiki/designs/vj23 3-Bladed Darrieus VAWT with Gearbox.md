## 3-Bladed Darrieus VAWT with Gearbox

This page covers the specific Darrieus-type VAWT sketched in `vj23`, which uses three blades and a gearbox-chain drive to the generator. (source: sources/vj23.md)

![Source figure](../../images/vj23-fig5.jpg)
Original caption: Figure 5. Vertical Axis Wind Turbine Type of Darrieus [[vj23|Source]]

- The source says the design uses `3` blades. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It places the gearbox on the turbine and connects it to the generator with a chain. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- The paper frames the basic working principle as wind turning the turbine, the gearbox increasing rotational speed, and the generator converting that motion into electricity. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It mentions gearbox use around `1:60` in the discussion. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- The abstract reports torque above `40 rpm` at wind speeds of `4.5 to 5 m/s` for the `3`-blade configuration. (source: sources/vj23.md)

> Uncertainty: the paper does not report enough whole-turbine geometric or performance data to populate most of the design catalog fields confidently. (source: sources/vj23.md)
