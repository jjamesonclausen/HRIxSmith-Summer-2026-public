## Eurowind VAWT

The Eurowind concept is a vertical-axis turbine design adapted for mounting on industrial chimneys and similar tall structures without inhibiting their normal use. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Geometry

- The rotor uses asymmetrical airfoil blades selected for high lift and low drag. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The blade is mounted to its supporting cross arm at a critical angle to optimize performance. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The blades are not twisted and have parallel edges, unlike twisted tapering HAWT blades. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The simple blade shape and lower VAWT blade stresses allow sectional mechanical production by extrusion or pultrusion. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The source shows small and large modular Eurowind concepts and a marine adaptation. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Performance

- The source says the turbine is self-starting. (source: sources/va3.md)
- It uses at least three blades to avoid the risk of reaching equilibrium during startup rotation. (source: sources/va3.md)
- It uses more than two blades to reduce cyclic loading and power pulsation/fluctuation. (source: sources/va3.md)
- It uses a direct-drive permanent-magnet generator, no gearbox, and only one moving part. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The source says lower blade rotational speeds imply lower noise levels. (source: sources/va3.md)
