## va30 Two-Bucket Semicircular Savonius Benchmark

This source provides a wind-tunnel benchmark family rather than one uniquely specified production turbine. Its two-bucket reference geometry consists of two `180-degree` semicircular buckets with radius `0.25 m`, nominal rotor diameter `1 m`, and tested bucket heights of `1 m` and `1.5 m`. (source: sources/va30.md)

- The directly shown `1 m` high two-bucket test series used gap ratios `S/D = 0`, `0.10`, `0.15`, and `0.20` at `7 m/s` (`Re/m = 4.32 x 10^5`). (source: sources/va30.md)
- Its plotted maximum cP is approximately `0.24` at TSR approximately `0.7` for the `S/D = 0.10-0.15` cases; this is read from Figure 7 rather than tabulated data. (source: sources/va30.md)
- Dynamic coefficients incorporate the measured friction tare torque and the authors' tunnel-blockage correction. (source: sources/va30.md)
