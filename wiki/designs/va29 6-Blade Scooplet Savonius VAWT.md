## va29 6-Blade Scooplet Savonius VAWT

This source-specific, six-blade circular-arc Savonius optimum uses three independent blade shapes and their 180-degree rotated counterparts. (source: sources/va29.md)

- At `AR = 1.5`, it reaches cP `0.321` at TSR `0.9`; Figure 18 reports a highest cP of `0.338` at `AR = 4`. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The baseline geometry has `D = 0.68 m`, `H = 1.02 m`, and swept area `0.6936 m^2`. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The authors report the gain over the four-blade optimum is nearly negligible and infer that adding more blades is unlikely to improve performance. (source: sources/va29.md)
- Compared with a multiple-quarter-blade rotor re-evaluated by the same CFD model at cP `0.20`, it reports a 60% cP improvement. (source: sources/va29.md)
