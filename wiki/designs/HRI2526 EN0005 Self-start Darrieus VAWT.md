## EN0005 Self-start Darrieus VAWT

This HRI prototype is an EN0005-based Darrieus concept selected as one of the four small-scale turbines for prototyping and wind-tunnel evaluation. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

## Geometry

- The report lists EN0005 as one of the four prototype concepts alongside the Classical Savonius, Helical Savonius within a Helical Darrieus, and H-Rotor Darrieus with Savonius Compartments. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Like the other HRI prototypes, it was modeled in CAD and 3D printed at a common prototype size of 5 inches tall by 4.5 inches wide so swept areas could be compared fairly. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The HRI team used this concept as a distinct low-wind-startup candidate during prototype screening. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- After the first wind-tunnel round, EN0005 remained in consideration while the Darrieus with Savonius Compartments concept was removed. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

## Performance

- EN0005 was one of the three configurations carried into the later prototype-optimization rounds. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- It was not the final selected concept; the Helical Hybrid achieved the highest overall evaluation score. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
