## Classical Savonius

The baseline Savonius form: a two-bladed, drag-based VAWT built from semicircular scoops. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - Two semicircular blades sit on opposite sides of a vertical shaft. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The concave side faces the wind on the driving half of the cycle. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Performance:
  - Self-starting and effective at low wind speeds. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - TSR stays between 0 and 1 because the rotor is drag-driven. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - Simple, low-cost construction and low noise are key benefits. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - Negative torque from the returning blade is the main efficiency penalty. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
