## D-rotor / Eggbeater Darrieus

A curved-blade Darrieus variant often called the eggbeater rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - Curved blades trace a troposkien-like form around the shaft. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The curvature changes the blade angle of attack through the cycle. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Performance:
  - It is lift-based and belongs to the Darrieus family. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The varying angle of attack can change self-starting and torque consistency relative to H-rotors. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - Curved geometry can be harder to manufacture than straight-bladed layouts. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Named in the report as one of the three main Darrieus types. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Curved blades give the rotor a varying angle of attack through the cycle. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
