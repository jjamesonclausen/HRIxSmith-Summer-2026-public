## Four-Bladed Helical VAWT

The `va4` study investigates a four-bladed helical vertical-axis wind turbine as its reference design for 2D LES and 3D U-RANS analysis. (source: sources/va4.md)

## Geometry

- Blade number: 4. (source: sources/va4.md)
- Blade profile: NACA 0018. (source: sources/va4.md)
- Blade phase angle change: 70 degrees. (source: sources/va4.md)
- Chord length: 0.1 m. (source: sources/va4.md)
- Rotor radius: 0.21 m. (source: sources/va4.md)
- Blade height: 0.54 m. (source: sources/va4.md)

![Source figure](va4-fig1.jpg)
Original caption: Figure 1. The sketch of HVAWT. [[va4|Source]]

## Unique Design Choices

- The shaft and support arms were simplified in simulation so the study could focus on blade behavior and reduce meshing cost. (source: sources/va4.md)

## Performance

- The study reports the best power coefficient near TSR 1.46 for the investigated case. (source: sources/va4.md)
- The source presents the helical layout as lower-noise and less fluctuation-prone than a straight-bladed rotor. (source: sources/va4.md)
