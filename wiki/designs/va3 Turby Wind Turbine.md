## Turby Wind Turbine

The Turby is a three-bladed helical vertical-axis turbine advocated for urban environments, with blades designed at a fixed distance from the vertical shaft. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Geometry

- Rotor diameter: 1999 mm. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rotor height: 2650 mm. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Overall height: 2890 mm. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Turbine weight including blades: 136 kg. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Number of blades: 3. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Blade material: composite. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Converter type: 4-quadrant AC-DC-AC. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Generator: three-phase synchronous permanent magnet. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Performance

- Cut-in wind speed: 4 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rated wind speed: 14 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Cut-out wind speed: 14 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Survival wind speed: 55 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rated rotational speed: 120-400 rpm. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rated blade speed: 42 m/s. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rated power at 14 m/s: 2.5 kW. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Peak power: 3.0 kW. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Starting is achieved by generator motor operation. (source: sources/va3.md)
