## va29 4-Blade Scooplet Savonius VAWT

This source-specific, four-blade circular-arc Savonius optimum uses two independent blade shapes and their 180-degree rotated counterparts. (source: sources/va29.md)

- At `AR = 1.5`, its reported cP is `0.318`; with increased AR, Figure 18 reports a maximum cP of `0.333` at `AR = 4`. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The corresponding initial geometry uses `D = 0.68 m`, `H = 1.02 m`, and swept area `0.6936 m^2`. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The global optimum resembles the earlier scooplet-based layout but uses flatter main blades; the matched 3D CFD comparison reports a 3% relative improvement. (source: sources/va29.md)
