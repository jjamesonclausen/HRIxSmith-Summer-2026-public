## Multi-stage Savonius within H-rotor Darrieus

A hybrid layout that places a multi-stage Savonius inside an H-rotor Darrieus frame. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - The design nests multiple Savonius stages inside a straight-bladed Darrieus frame. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Performance:
  - It is presented as one of the candidate hybrid concepts in the report's concept table. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - The nested layout adds complexity and may increase blockage or aerodynamic interaction. > Inference. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Named in the HRI report as one of the example hybrid configurations. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- It combines the low-speed torque of Savonius with the lift-based structure of an H-rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
