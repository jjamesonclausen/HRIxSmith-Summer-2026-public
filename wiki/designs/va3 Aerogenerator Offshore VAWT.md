## Aerogenerator Offshore VAWT

The Aerogenerator is an offshore V-shaped vertical-axis turbine concept described as 144 m high and capable of generating up to 9 MW. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Geometry

- Height: 144 m. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Structure: V-shaped, with two arms jutting from the base. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rigid sails are mounted along the arms at intervals and act like airfoils as wind passes over them. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The concept is intended to keep its center of gravity near the bottom even as the structure scales upward. (source: sources/va3.md)

![Figure 30. Offshore vertical Aerogenerator concept. Photo: Grimshaw Architects.](../../images/va3-fig30.jpg)

## Performance

- Rated power described as up to 9 MW. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The source says the concept can produce roughly three times as much power as a conventional turbine of equivalent size. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Rotation speed is described as roughly 3 rpm. (source: sources/va3.md)
