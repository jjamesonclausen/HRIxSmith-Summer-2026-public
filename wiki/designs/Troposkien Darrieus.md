## Troposkien Darrieus

A curved-blade Darrieus design shaped to follow the troposkien form. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - The blades follow a curved troposkien profile rather than a straight span. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The curved shape helps manage loading near the top and bottom of the rotor. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- Performance:
  - It is one of the Darrieus families the HRI report considered in its type map. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - The shape is aerodynamically meaningful but more structurally and manufacturing-wise complex than straight blades. (source: sources/vj4.md)

- Named in the report as a Darrieus rotor family distinct from H-rotor and eggbeater layouts. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- The curved blade shape is associated with large turbines and structural/aerodynamic tradeoffs near the top and bottom of the rotor. (source: sources/vj4.md)
