## Helical Hybrid

A hybrid VAWT combining helical Darrieus blades with an internal helical Savonius rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md, sources/vj8.md)

- Geometry:
  - The final HRI design uses asymmetrical helical Darrieus blades around a widened internal helical Savonius rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The rotor overlap ratio is optimized as part of the final geometry. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Performance:
  - The HRI report selected this concept after screening more than 200 concepts and testing four prototypes. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - In the report, it achieved the highest evaluation score and was carried forward as the chosen configuration. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - It aims to balance low-speed startup with higher-speed lift-based output. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The design is more complex than either a pure Savonius or pure Darrieus rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
