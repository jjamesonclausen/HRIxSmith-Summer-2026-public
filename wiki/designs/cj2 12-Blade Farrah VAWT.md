# cj2 12-Blade Farrah VAWT

The Farrah VAWT has 12 profiled blades around an inner cylinder, with endplates and a variable blade-pitch configuration. The paper evaluates positive and negative 7-, 15-, 20-, and 40-degree pitch settings. (source: sources/cj2.md)

The reported turbine height is 237 mm and the endplate diameter is 405 mm. The source does not provide a whole-rotor performance figure suitable for the required design properties. (source: sources/cj2.md)

> Uncertainty: the study’s power comparison includes assumed mechanical losses and uses a half-turbine symmetry model. It should not be used as an unadjusted power specification. (source: sources/cj2.md)
