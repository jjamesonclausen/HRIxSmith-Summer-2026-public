## Scooplet-Based Savonius

The `vj9` source's main novel design is a scooplet-based Savonius rotor built from simple circular arcs and optimized for higher power coefficient. (source: sources/vj9.md)

## Geometry

- The scooplet-based design removes overlap between the opposite blades. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The main scoop has radius 226 mm and spans 106 degrees. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The scooplet has radius 144 mm and spans 102 degrees. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The outermost point of the scooplet is 105 mm from the scoop at one end and decreases to about 65 mm. (source: sources/vj9.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The source emphasizes that the design uses only straight lines and circular-arc segments of constant thickness so it remains easy to manufacture. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The scooplet adds positive torque through the full rotation range while generating practically no negative torque of its own. (source: sources/vj9.md)

## Performance

- The source reports a 39% increase in power coefficient relative to the classical Savonius reference. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- It also reports that the scooplet-based design outperformed the next-best Bach-type design by 27% in peak power coefficient. (source: sources/vj9.md)
