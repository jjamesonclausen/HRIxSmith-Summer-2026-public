## Curved-Blade Savonius VAWT

The `vj19` paper advances one small-scale curved-blade Savonius VAWT after comparing four blade-shape options experimentally. (source: sources/vj19.md)

![Source figure](vj19-fig1a.jpg)
Original caption: Fig. 1a. A side view of designed and assembled blade configuration of VAWT. [[vj19|Source]]

![Source figure](vj19-fig1b.jpg)
Original caption: Fig. 1b. A block diagram of experimental measurement setup. [[vj19|Source]]

- The rotor uses three flattened trapezoidal galvanized-steel blades attached with `120` degree spacing. (source: sources/vj19.md)
- Reported blade dimensions are `0.8 m` width and `1.3 m` height, with `1.5 m` total turbine height. (source: sources/vj19.md)
- The structure uses a mild-steel hub and shaft, two bearings, an AC permanent-magnet generator, a rectifier, and `12 V DC` battery dump load instrumentation. (source: sources/vj19.md)
- The authors frame the design goal as low cut-in speed, low weight, portability, and suitability for non-directional low-speed urban wind. (source: sources/vj19.md)
- The measured/model-comparison point reported in the paper is `327.5 W` measured versus `367.2 W` modeled at `7 m/s`. (source: sources/vj19.md)
- The paper reports annual energy output of `7838 kWh`, annual revenue of `846.51 USD`, cut-in at `3 m/s`, cut-out at `17 m/s`, and a simple payback of `3.5 years` assuming `3000 USD` total system cost. (source: sources/vj19.md)

> Discrepancy: the text says the turbine produces rated power at `9 m/s`, but Table 2's electrical-power column continues increasing above `9 m/s`. The source therefore supports the rated-speed claim, but not a single source-clear rated-power value. (source: sources/vj19.md)
