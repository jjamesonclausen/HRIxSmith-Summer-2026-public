## QuietRevolution QR5

The QR5 is a lift-driven Darrieus-type VAWT chosen in `va1` as one of the two candidate urban turbines for rooftop evaluation in Trieste. (source: sources/va1.md)

## Geometry

- Height: 5 m. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Maximum diameter: 3.1 m. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Swept area: 13.6 m2. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Blade material: carbon fiber. (source: sources/va1.md)

## Unique Design Choices

- The source says the QR5 is designed specifically for environments close to people and buildings. (source: sources/va1.md)
- It is described as quieter because of limited blade tip speed ratio and blade sweeping. (source: sources/va1.md)

## Performance

- Rated power: 6 kW. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Cut-off wind speed: 19 m/s. (source: sources/va1.md)
- The source notes that QR5 has a 33% higher `Pnom/A` value than the WS-12, which it uses as an indicator of the lift-driven concept's higher efficiency. (source: sources/va1.md)
