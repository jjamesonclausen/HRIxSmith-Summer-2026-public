## Blade Airfoil

This `vj21` paper treats blade airfoil as one of the most critical design parameters for a smaller-capacity fixed-pitch straight-bladed VAWT. (source: sources/vj21.md)

## Parameter

- The source contrasts the common use of symmetric NACA 4-digit airfoils like `NACA 0012`, `0015`, and `0018` with asymmetric low-speed airfoils. (source: sources/vj21.md)
- It says the main problem with the conventionally used symmetric airfoils is low starting torque at low speed. (source: sources/vj21.md)
- The paper cites earlier work by the same research group identifying desirable aerodynamic and geometric features for smaller-capacity SB-VAWT airfoils. (source: sources/vj21.md)

## Outcome

- The source states that the conventionally adopted symmetric NACA 4-digit airfoils are not suitable for smaller-capacity SB-VAWTs. (source: sources/vj21.md)
- It recommends a high-lift, low-drag, asymmetric thick airfoil for the low-speed operating regime typically encountered by these turbines. (source: sources/vj21.md)
- It also cites the `MI-VAWT1` special-purpose airfoil as outperforming `NACA 0015` at low Reynolds number. (source: sources/vj21.md)
