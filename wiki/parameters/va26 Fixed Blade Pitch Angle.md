## Fixed Blade Pitch Angle

This `va26` study varies fixed blade pitch angle on a 3-bladed H-type VAWT to improve aerodynamic performance at `TSR = 4`. (source: sources/va26.md)

- The tested pitch range is `-7` degrees to `+3` degrees, with `1` degree increments around the optimum. (source: sources/va26.md)
- The reported optimum is `-2` degrees, where `Cp` is about `6.6%` higher than at zero pitch. (source: sources/va26.md)
- The same `-2` degrees case also raises `CT` by about `2%` and produces an almost symmetric wake (`CY approx 0`). (source: sources/va26.md)
- Positive pitch values beyond zero quickly become harmful. The paper reports that `+3` degrees causes deep stall and about `66.5%` lower `Cp` than zero pitch. (source: sources/va26.md)
- More negative values help parts of the cycle, but `-7` degrees already reduces moment in the downwind region because of stronger flow separation. (source: sources/va26.md)
- The source frames the main mechanism as an azimuthal redistribution of instantaneous moment: positive pitch helps part of the fore half, negative pitch helps more of the rest of the revolution. (source: sources/va26.md)
- The paper therefore treats fixed pitch as useful but limited, and uses the shifted instantaneous loading as the main argument for future dynamic pitching. (source: sources/va26.md)
