## va30 Rotor Height

The test program varied bucket height between `1 m` and `1.5 m` with nominal rotor diameter `1 m`. (source: sources/va30.md)

- The authors conclude that aerodynamic performance increased slightly with height-to-diameter ratio. (source: sources/va30.md)
- The reported tunnel-blockage correction factor changed from `0.0125` for the `1 m` rotor to `0.0162` for the `1.5 m` rotor. (source: sources/va30.md)
