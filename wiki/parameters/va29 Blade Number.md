## va29 Blade Number

The study globally optimized separate two-, four-, and six-blade circular-arc Savonius designs to increase average cP. (source: sources/va29.md)

- At `AR = 1.5`, cP rises from `0.242` for two blades to `0.318` for four and `0.321` for six. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The four-to-six-blade difference is small, so the authors infer that further blade-number increases would not yield meaningful extra performance. (source: sources/va29.md)
- This conclusion is bounded by the paper's circular-arc parameterization, its optimization ranges, and the stated CFD model; it does not establish a universal blade-count optimum. (source: sources/va29.md)
