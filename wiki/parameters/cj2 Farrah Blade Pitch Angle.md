# cj2 Farrah Blade Pitch Angle

The study tests positive and negative pitch angles of 7, 15, 20, and 40 degrees on the 12-blade Farrah VAWT. (source: sources/cj2.md)

The paper reports that power increases with pitch angle and wind speed in its simulated and experimental trend comparison, and that negative pitch produces slightly higher power output. (source: sources/cj2.md)

At 11.7 m/s, the 7-degree cases could not rotate experimentally. At 16.8 m/s, the 40-degree cases generate the highest torque among the studied pitch angles. (source: sources/cj2.md)

> Uncertainty: the comparison depends on assumed mechanical losses and the paper recommends a transition SST model for more reliable power prediction across the full range. (source: sources/cj2.md)
