## va29 Rotor Aspect Ratio

The source varied rotor aspect ratio, defined as `AR = H/D`, after optimizing the circular-arc blade shapes and TSR. (source: sources/va29.md)

- cP was near maximum around `AR = 3`; further increases gave negligible benefit for the three optimized designs. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The optimized six-blade cP rose from `0.320` at `AR = 1.5` to `0.338` at `AR = 4`. (source: sources/va29.md)
- From `AR = 1` to `AR = 2.5`, the four- and six-blade designs gained 15% cP, while the two-blade design gained 5%. (source: sources/va29.md)
- For `AR < 1`, cP dropped significantly for all cases. (source: sources/va29.md)
