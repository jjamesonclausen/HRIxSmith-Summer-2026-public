## Pitch Amplitude

The `vj15` study varies harmonic pitch amplitude in an H-type Darrieus turbine and tests `0`, `3`, `10`, and `20` degree amplitudes. (source: sources/vj15.md)

## Parameter

- The best reported amplitude is `3` degrees. (source: sources/vj15.md)
- Larger amplitudes reduce useful operating range and can worsen performance at higher TSR. (source: sources/vj15.md)
- The paper reports about a `34%` power increase relative to fixed pitch for the best case. (source: sources/vj15.md)

## Outcome

- Use a small amplitude if the goal is to improve both power and startup without over-reducing angle of attack. (source: sources/vj15.md)
- Treat larger amplitudes as a risk for over-correction and drag-dominated behavior. (source: sources/vj15.md)
