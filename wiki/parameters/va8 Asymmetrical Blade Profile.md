## Asymmetrical Blade Profile

This `va8` patent changes the blade cross-section from a conventional profile to an asymmetrical profile with unequal upper and lower cambers. (source: sources/va8.md)

## Parameter Change

- The blade profile is asymmetrical and is designed to create a large but smoothly generated path difference between upper and lower streamlines. (source: sources/va8.md)
- The claimed profile characteristics are: 20% path difference between upper and lower surfaces as a fraction of chord, 3% path difference between chord line and lower surface, and 11.5% maximum-thickness-to-chord ratio. (source: sources/va8.md)

## Outcome

- The patent claims the profile provides high starting torque even at low wind speeds. (source: sources/va8.md)
- Wind-tunnel tests in the patent say the profile generates high lift coefficients of similar magnitude for wind from either direction. (source: sources/va8.md)
- The worked example reports 2.5 times the net circumferential force of a typical flat-bottom profile. (source: sources/va8.md)
