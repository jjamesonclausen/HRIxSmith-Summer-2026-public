## Wind Deflector Shape

This `vj27` review compares several wind-deflector geometries as passive augmentation devices for small VAWTs. (source: sources/vj27.md)

## Parameter

- The review groups deflector geometry into flat-plate, airfoil-shaped, and compound structures. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- Flat-plate deflectors are presented as the simplest and cheapest option, while airfoil-shaped and compound layouts are treated as higher-performance but more complex alternatives. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- The compound category includes layouts such as V-shaped deflectors and kite-shaped deflectors for twin-turbine arrangements. (source: sources/vj27.md)

## Outcome

- The review reports about `27.3%` gain for a two-bladed Savonius flat-plate case and `27.55%` for a three-bladed flat-plate case. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- It reports up to `50%` coefficient-of-performance improvement for one airfoil-shaped Savonius deflector case, which is the highest single gain in the paper's comparison table. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- For compound layouts, the review reports about `26%` gain for a flat-plate twin-turbine case and up to `38.6%` for a kite-shaped twin-turbine case. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- The paper therefore treats deflector shape as a first-order performance lever rather than a cosmetic detail, with more aerodynamic shapes generally outperforming a simple flat plate when the rest of the setup is favorable. (source: sources/vj27.md)
