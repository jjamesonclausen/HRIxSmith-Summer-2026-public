## Wind Flow Modifier

This `va20` study adds a directional wind flow modifier (`WFM`) ahead of the involute rotor to accelerate low-speed urban wind before it reaches the turbine. (source: sources/va20.md)

- The WFM uses `12` diffuser tubes arranged `4 x 3`, with shrinking tube diameter from `0.3 m` to `0.15 m` toward the turbine. (source: sources/va20.md)
- The paper reports pressure change inside the tubes from about `+465 Pa` to `-19.3 Pa`, which drives outlet-flow acceleration. (source: sources/va20.md)
- Reported flow speed rises from `1.822 m/s` at the diffuser inlet to `5.562 m/s` at the outlet, corresponding to velocity magnification ratio `3.05`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- Relative to the involute rotor without WFM, the WFM-assisted case increases the low-speed lift-to-drag ratio from `14.02` to `18.44` at `60 rpm`, and raises it as high as `31.44` at `250 rpm`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- The reported maximum power coefficient improves from `0.22` to `0.397` at `5 m/s`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- The paper also reports maximum turbine power rising from `951.31 W` to `1361.4 W` at `250 rpm` / `21 m/s`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- The source still limits this result to the proposed wind direction and explicitly suggests circular WFM geometry as future work for omnidirectional use. (source: sources/va20.md)
