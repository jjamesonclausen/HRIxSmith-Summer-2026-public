## va30 Gap Ratio

The study varied the dimensionless inside-edge gap ratio `S/D` on two-bucket Savonius rotors. (source: sources/va30.md)

- The `1 m` high, two-bucket tests compared `S/D = 0`, `0.10`, `0.15`, and `0.20` at `Re/m = 4.32 x 10^5`. (source: sources/va30.md)
- The data indicate the strongest performance at `S/D = 0.10-0.15`; both smaller and larger gaps decreased cP, particularly at higher TSR. (source: sources/va30.md)
- The authors give the same range as their recommended gap for maximum power from a fixed bucket size. (source: sources/va30.md)
