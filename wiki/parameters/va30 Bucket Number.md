## va30 Bucket Number

The paper compared two- and three-bucket Savonius configurations. (source: sources/va30.md)

- The two-bucket configuration had a maximum cP approximately 1.5 times that of the three-bucket configuration in the reported `S/D = 0.15`, `H = 1 m`, `Re/m = 8.64 x 10^5` comparison. (source: sources/va30.md)
- Three buckets reduced the variation in static torque with angular position, giving higher minimum static torque in the reported cases. (source: sources/va30.md)
- The authors recommend two offset two-bucket stages rather than a three-bucket rotor where startup torque must be improved without sacrificing the two-bucket performance advantage. (source: sources/va30.md)
