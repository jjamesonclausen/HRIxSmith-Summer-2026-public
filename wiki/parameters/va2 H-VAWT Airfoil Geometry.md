## H-VAWT Airfoil Geometry

This `va2` study changes H-VAWT airfoil geometry by optimizing six CST shape coefficients together with installation angle relative to a NACA0015 baseline. (source: sources/va2.md)

## Parameter Change

- The initial reference airfoil is NACA0015. (source: sources/va2.md)
- Seven design variables are used: `a1`, `a2`, `a5`, `b1`, `b2`, `b5`, and installation angle `α`. (source: sources/va2.md)
- The source identifies leading-edge radius, relative thickness, trailing-edge shape, and installation angle as the main coordinated design changes. (source: sources/va2.md)

## Outcome

- Optimized airfoil 1 improved power coefficient by 14.2% relative to NACA0015 under the selected optimization condition. (source: sources/va2.md)
- The source also reports that optimized airfoil 2 improves power coefficient by `11.6%` relative to the baseline under the same optimization condition. (source: sources/va2.md)
- Across the full operating range, the source reports an average `9.8%` increase in power coefficient. (source: sources/va2.md)
- The paper ranks leading-edge radius as the most influential geometric driver, followed by relative thickness and trailing-edge shape. (source: sources/va2.md)
- The paper attributes the gain to delayed flow separation, lower drag, and improved torque generation, rather than to a single isolated shape change. (source: sources/va2.md)
