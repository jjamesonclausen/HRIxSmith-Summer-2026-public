## Pitch Angle

This `vj29` review identifies pitch angle as one of the main parameters shaping H-type Darrieus aerodynamic performance. (source: sources/vj29.md)

- The review cites work showing that pitch angle and solidity both affect aerodynamic performance to a high extent in self-starting VAWT analysis. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- It reports one cited study where the best performance was achieved when pitch angle was zero in that specific comparison. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- The review also says pitch-angle changes redistribute instantaneous moment between the upwind and downwind parts of the cycle, which is why it presents dynamic pitching as a promising route for further optimization. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- In its research-gap discussion, the paper again treats active and passive control methods as important because poor startup and dynamic-stall losses remain central H-rotor weaknesses. (source: sources/vj29.md)
