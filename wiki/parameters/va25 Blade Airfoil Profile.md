## Blade Airfoil Profile

This `va25` study compares multiple blade airfoil profiles on the same straight-bladed Darrieus rotor to improve low-TSR self-starting and overall aerodynamic efficiency. (source: sources/va25.md)

- The symmetric profiles studied are `NACA0021` (reference), `E474`, and `S1048`. (source: sources/va25.md)
- The cambered profiles studied are `NACA6712`, `DU-06-W-200`, `Clark Y`, `S1210`, and `FX63-137`. (source: sources/va25.md)
- For low TSRs, cambered-in `NACA6712` performs best, reaching `Cp = 0.3645` at `TSR = 2.037` and about `180%` more `Cp` than the reference airfoil at the same TSR. (source: sources/va25.md)
- For mid-to-high TSRs, symmetric `E474` performs best, reaching `Cp = 0.3557` at `TSR = 3.0` and about `19.5%` higher `Cp` than the reference at that TSR. (source: sources/va25.md)
- `DU-06-W-200` also performs well over a broad higher-TSR range, but not as strongly as `E474` overall. (source: sources/va25.md)
- `S1210`, `Clark Y`, and `FX63-137` in cambered-in orientation perform poorly across most TSR ranges. (source: sources/va25.md)
- The source therefore recommends `NACA6712` for low-TSR applications and `E474` for mid-to-high TSR operation. (source: sources/va25.md)
