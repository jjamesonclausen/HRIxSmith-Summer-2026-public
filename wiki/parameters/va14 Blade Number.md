## Blade Number

The `va14` study compares 2-, 3-, and 4-bladed Darrieus H-type VAWTs to evaluate how blade number changes loads, wake, and performance. (source: sources/va14.md)

## Parameter

- The paper studies turbines with 2, 3, and 4 blades. (source: sources/va14.md)
- It compares them at fixed Reynolds number and identical solidity and tip-speed ratio to isolate blade-number effects. (source: sources/va14.md)

![Source figure](va14-fig16.jpg)
Original caption: Fig. 16. Power coefficients versus tip speed ratio and solidity for 2-, 3-, and 4-bladed VAWTs with fixed Rec at identical l e s positions (TI = 5%). [[va14|Source]]

## Outcome

- Near the turbine optimal operating range, the paper reports that power coefficient is almost independent of blade number. (source: sources/va14.md)
- It therefore frames blade number as more important for smoothness of output, structural loads, vibrations, and cost than for peak `Cp` near `λopt`. (source: sources/va14.md)
- At lower tip-speed ratios, blade number still changes dynamic-stall behavior and load fluctuation scale through its effect on reduced frequency and blade chord. (source: sources/va14.md)
