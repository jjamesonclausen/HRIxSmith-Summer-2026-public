## Blade Count for VAWT Startup and Pulsation

The source treats blade count as a design parameter in the Eurowind and Venturi discussions, linking additional blades to startup reliability, torque, and reduced cyclic loading. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Parameter

- The Eurowind section says using 3 blades or more eliminates the risk of blades reaching equilibrium during startup rotation. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The same section says using more than 2 blades reduces cyclic loading and power pulsation/fluctuation. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The Venturi section says the six-bladed rotor induces a high startup moment. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Outcome

- The source presents increased blade count as beneficial for startup and smoothness in these specific designs. (source: sources/va3.md)
- In Eurowind, three or more blades are linked to startup reliability, and more than two blades are linked to lower cyclic loading and power fluctuation. (source: sources/va3.md)
- In Venturi, six blades are linked to high startup moment and low downtime. (source: sources/va3.md)
