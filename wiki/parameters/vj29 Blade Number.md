## Blade Number

This `vj29` review treats blade number as a meaningful H-type Darrieus startup parameter. (source: sources/vj29.md)

- The review includes blade number among the main design elements controlling H-type Darrieus aerodynamic performance. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- It cites a startup study where larger blade counts helped attain higher power coefficients at `TSR < 1`, improving self-starting ability. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- The same review still treats blade number as a tradeoff variable rather than a free gain, because it repeatedly emphasizes the interaction between blade count, TSR, solidity, and unsteady loading. (source: sources/vj29.md)
