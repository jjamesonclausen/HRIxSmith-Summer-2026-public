## Rotor Blade Profile

This `va20` study changes the blade profile from a drag-oriented `C-shaped` blade to an involute blade to improve low-wind urban VAWT performance. (source: sources/va20.md)

- The source frames the involute shape as a way to add strong lift support while still retaining useful drag from the blade's interior volume. (source: sources/va20.md)
- At `60 rpm` / `5 m/s`, the baseline C-blade case reports net `Cd = 0.205`, net `Cl = -0.094`, and a drag-to-lift ratio of `2.181`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- At the same low-speed point, the involute case reports net `Cl = -0.5524`, net `Cd = -0.0375`, and a lift-to-drag ratio of `14.02`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- The source says this force-ratio shift indicates that the involute blades rotate mainly by lift rather than drag and give positive moments on all three blades. (source: sources/va20.md)
- The reported maximum power coefficient rises from `0.071` for the C-blade rotor to `0.22` for the involute rotor at `5 m/s`. (source: sources/va20.md)
- At the highest tested speed, reported turbine power rises from `188.9 W` to `951.31 W`. (source: sources/va20.md)
