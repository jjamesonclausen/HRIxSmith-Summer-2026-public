## Airfoil Camber Line

This `vj4` study compares a symmetrical NACA0018 airfoil with the cambered DU 06-W-200 airfoil on otherwise similar H-Darrieus rotors. (source: sources/vj4.md)

## Parameter Change

- The source compares NACA0018 with the DU 06-W-200 airfoil, which is 20% thick and has 0.8% camber. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The comparison is used to test whether a cambered DU profile can improve low-TSR behavior relative to a more conventional symmetrical small-VAWT airfoil. (source: sources/vj4.md)

## Outcome

- The DU profile gives better performance at starting TSRs and can mitigate the poor self-starting behavior of Darrieus turbines. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- In the source's Table 3 comparison, the DU 06-W-200 case has `TSRopt = 3.4` and `Vopt = 9.2 m/s`, compared with `TSRopt = 3.9` and `Vopt = 8.1 m/s` for the NACA0018 case. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The same source notes that the DU-profile rotor underperforms the NACA0018 case at high TSRs and is therefore less attractive where medium-low urban winds need stronger high-TSR exploitation. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The paper treats the cambered profile as a startup-oriented tradeoff: it helps low-TSR behavior, but gives up some high-TSR efficiency. (source: sources/vj4.md)
