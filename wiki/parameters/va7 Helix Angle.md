## Helix Angle

This `va7` study changes the blade helix angle of a helical VAWT to investigate how it affects power output, wake structure, and load smoothness. (source: sources/va7.md)

## Parameter Change

- The study compares helix angles of 60 degrees, 90 degrees, and 120 degrees. (source: sources/va7.md)
- The rest of the baseline geometry is held constant: NACA0015 blades, no pitch, 210 mm chord, 1 m diameter, and 3 m height. (source: sources/va7.md)

## Outcome

- The source reports the best power performance at 60 degrees helix angle. (source: sources/va7.md)
- Increasing helix angle reduces the standard deviation of `Cp`, producing smoother cyclic loading. (source: sources/va7.md)
- The parameter therefore gives a strong smoothing benefit, but with a tradeoff against peak power at larger helix angles. (source: sources/va7.md)
