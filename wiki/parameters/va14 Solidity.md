## Solidity

The `va14` study treats solidity as a core aerodynamic design variable for Darrieus H-type VAWTs. (source: sources/va14.md)

## Parameter

- Solidity is studied from 0.09 to 0.36 across 2-, 3-, and 4-bladed turbines. (source: sources/va14.md)
- The paper reports that increasing solidity lowers the optimal tip-speed ratio. (source: sources/va14.md)
- It also introduces `sλ^3` as an approximately invariant parameter for `λopt` across the tested cases. (source: sources/va14.md)

![Source figure](../../images/va14-fig2.jpg)
Original caption: Fig. 2. Power coefficient versus (a) tip speed ratio and (b) solidity for 2-bladed VAWTs (TI = 5%). [[va14|Source]]

## Outcome

- For constant-speed urban VAWTs, where operation often occurs at moderate to high tip-speed ratio, the paper says relatively low solidity is optimal. (source: sources/va14.md)
- For variable-speed VAWTs, it says a moderately high-solidity rotor operating at relatively low tip-speed ratio is optimal. (source: sources/va14.md)
- The study also shows that some apparent `Cp` gain from higher solidity disappears when Reynolds number is held constant, so solidity should not be treated as a simple one-way performance booster. (source: sources/va14.md)
