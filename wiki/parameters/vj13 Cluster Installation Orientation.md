## Cluster Installation Orientation

The source varies the direction of the entire three-turbine cluster without changing the internal layout. (source: sources/vj13.md)

- The paper defines orientation with an arrow line perpendicular to the center line of T1 and T3. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The best orientation depends on the site's wind-direction energy distribution, not just the prevailing wind direction. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- Site 1 performs best when oriented east, Site 2 when oriented southeast, and Site 3 when oriented west. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The reported maximum annual output ratios are about `1.25`, `1.27`, and `1.16/1.17` relative to the isolated turbine. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- Minimum values stay well above the worst single-direction case, showing that multi-direction annual evaluation matters. (source: sources/vj13.md)
