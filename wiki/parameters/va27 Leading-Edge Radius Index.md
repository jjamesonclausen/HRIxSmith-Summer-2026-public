## Leading-Edge Radius Index

This `va27` study varies the leading-edge radius index `I` to understand how sharper or blunter symmetric airfoils affect VAWT power performance under deep dynamic stall. (source: sources/va27.md)

- The tested leading-edge radius indices are `4.5`, `6.0`, and `7.5`, where `I = 6` corresponds to the original NACA shape family. (source: sources/va27.md)
- In general, decreasing `I` from `6.0` to `4.5` monotonically increases turbine `CP`, regardless of the studied `t/c` and `xt/c` combinations. (source: sources/va27.md)
- The source says the improvement becomes strongest near the locally optimal combinations of thickness and thickness position. (source: sources/va27.md)
- For moderate-to-thick airfoils, the paper reports much higher sensitivity to `I` than for thin airfoils. (source: sources/va27.md)
- The study identifies the global optima at both `lambda = 2.5` and `3.0` with the smallest tested leading-edge radius index, `I = 4.5`. (source: sources/va27.md)
- The source attributes the improvement mainly to a reduction in the drag jump associated with stall. (source: sources/va27.md)
