## Blade Number

This `vj23` paper treats blade count as one of the main design choices affecting turbine effectiveness in low wind. (source: sources/vj23.md)

## Parameter

- The abstract singles out a `3`-blade turbine and reports torque above `40 rpm` at `4.5 to 5 m/s`. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- Later in the results section, the paper compares `3`-blade and `4`-blade behavior qualitatively. (source: sources/vj23.md)

## Outcome

- The source says a `4`-blade turbine has greater total torque and a more stable torque graph profile than the `3`-blade case. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It also says the more blades used, the more effective the turbine is to catch the wind. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- But the same section warns that adding more blades also increases "blocking" by blades with small or negative torque, which reduces performance. (source: sources/vj23.md)

> Discrepancy: the paper does not cleanly resolve whether the preferred design is the abstract's `3`-blade case or the later-discussed `4`-blade torque advantage. (source: sources/vj23.md)
