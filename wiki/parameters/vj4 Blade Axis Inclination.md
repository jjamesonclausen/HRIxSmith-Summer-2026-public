## Blade Axis Inclination

This `vj4` study changes blade-axis inclination by comparing a straight H-shaped rotor with a helical twisted-blade rotor. (source: sources/vj4.md)

## Parameter Change

- The source compares case C, an H-shaped straight-bladed rotor, with case D, a helical twisted-blade configuration. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The comparison isolates the effect of twisting the blade axis rather than changing the overall small-VAWT concept. (source: sources/vj4.md)

## Outcome

- The twisted geometry ensures there is always a blade section at each possible angle of attack, averaging and smoothing load oscillations. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The source's Table 3 comparison shows strong load-smoothing: `RQ` falls from `0.29` for the straight case to `0.16` for the helical case, and `RTx` falls from `0.17` to `0.06`. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The helical configuration keeps a similar optimum operating point, with `TSRopt = 4.0` versus `3.9` for the straight case, but gives a broader near-peak operating band. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The paper judges the helical arrangement as the improved rotor architecture overall, but still notes the expected cost and manufacturing penalty relative to the straight-bladed case. (source: sources/vj4.md)
