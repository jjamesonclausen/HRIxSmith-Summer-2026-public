## Blade Axis Inclination

This `vj4` study changes blade-axis inclination by comparing a straight H-shaped rotor with a helical twisted-blade rotor. (source: sources/vj4.md)

## Parameter Change

- The source compares case C, an H-shaped straight-bladed rotor, with case D, a helical twisted-blade configuration. (source: sources/vj4.md)

## Outcome

- The twisted geometry ensures there is always a blade section at each possible angle of attack, averaging and smoothing load oscillations. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The helical configuration shows a wider TSR range near maximum power coefficient and is judged the improved rotor architecture in the study. (source: sources/vj4.md)
