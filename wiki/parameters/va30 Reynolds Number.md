## va30 Reynolds Number

The paper tested nominal freestream velocities of `7 m/s` and `14 m/s`, corresponding to `Re/m = 4.32 x 10^5` and `8.67 x 10^5`. (source: sources/va30.md)

- The authors conclude that increasing test Reynolds number generally improves aerodynamic performance. (source: sources/va30.md)
- They attribute the observed static-torque-coefficient shift with higher Reynolds number to delayed separation around the buckets. (source: sources/va30.md)
