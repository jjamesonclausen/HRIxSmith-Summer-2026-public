## Airfoil Relative Maximum Thickness

This `va27` study varies symmetric-airfoil relative maximum thickness `t/c` to understand how blade thickness changes VAWT performance in deep dynamic stall. (source: sources/va27.md)

- The tested thickness values are `10%`, `12%`, `15%`, `18%`, `21%`, and `24%`. (source: sources/va27.md)
- At `lambda = 2.5`, thicker airfoils generally perform better, and the optimum thickness shifts upward as the maximum-thickness position moves downstream. (source: sources/va27.md)
- For `I = 4.5`, the study reports `topt/c` values increasing from `12%` to `21%` as `xt/c` moves from `20%` to `30%`. (source: sources/va27.md)
- At `lambda = 3.0`, the sensitivity of `CP` to `t/c` becomes even stronger because the blade spends less of the cycle in fully separated post-stall flow. (source: sources/va27.md)
- The reported global optimum at `lambda = 2.5` uses `t/c = 24%`, while the optimum at `lambda = 3.0` uses `t/c = 18%`. (source: sources/va27.md)
- The source therefore treats thickness as beneficial up to an operating-condition-dependent optimum, after which earlier or stronger stall and reduced `Cl-alpha` slope begin to hurt performance. (source: sources/va27.md)
