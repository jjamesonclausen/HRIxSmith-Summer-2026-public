## Blade Profile

This `vj22` study compares two symmetric NACA blade profiles for a low-wind-speed straight-bladed VAWT using QBlade simulation. (source: sources/vj22.md)

## Parameter

- The paper compares `NACA 0012` and `NACA 0015`. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- Both profiles have zero camber, with thicknesses `12` and `15` respectively. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- The comparison is based on `Cl`, `Cd`, `Cl/Cd`, output power, and later rotor `Cp` behavior. (source: sources/vj22.md)

## Outcome

- The source says `NACA 0015` performs better than `NACA 0012` in terms of `Cl/Cd` over most of the studied Reynolds-number range. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- It reports that `NACA 0015` gives higher `Clmax` than `NACA 0012` in the shown `Re = 50000` comparison and produces higher output power at higher wind speed. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- The paper concludes that `NACA 0015` is the better-performing airfoil of the two for this low-wind-speed VAWT study. (source: sources/vj22.md)
