## Turbine Spacing in Paired VAWT Arrays

The study varies center-to-center spacing to determine how closely interacting turbines can be placed before wake losses dominate. (source: sources/va12.md)

## Parameter

- Co-rotating arrays were tested at 1.25 D, 1.5 D, 2 D, and 3 D spacing. (source: sources/va12.md)
- The detailed three-dimensional flow study then focused on 1.5 D spacing because it preserved strong interaction while still resolving the accelerated flow region between rotors. (source: sources/va12.md)

## Outcome

- Broad enhancement was strongest at the smallest spacing and decayed with increasing spacing. (source: sources/va12.md)
- In one negative-angle regime, the maximum downstream enhancement occurred at 1.5 D rather than the smallest spacing. (source: sources/va12.md)
- The paper therefore supports close spacing, but the best spacing depends on which interaction regime and performance goal matter most. (source: sources/va12.md)
