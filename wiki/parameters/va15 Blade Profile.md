## Blade Profile

The `va15` study compares three typical small-scale H-Darrieus blade profiles for startup and performance. (source: sources/va15.md)

## Parameter

- The study compares NACA0021, DU06W200, and NACA4415. (source: sources/va15.md)
- All tested blades use the same chord length of 100 mm in the reported cases. (source: sources/va15.md)

![Source figure](va15-table2.jpg)
Original caption: TABLE 2 Testing parameters for studies of turbine performance with different blade profiles [[va15|Source]]

## Outcome

- The DU06W200 profile gives the highest peak `Cp`. (source: sources/va15.md)
- The NACA0021 profile gives better self-starting because it produces more power at low tip speed ratio. (source: sources/va15.md)
- In this setup, the cambered NACA4415 profile failed to self-start under all tested conditions. (source: sources/va15.md)
