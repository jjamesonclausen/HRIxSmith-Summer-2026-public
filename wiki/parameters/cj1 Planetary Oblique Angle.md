# cj1 Planetary Oblique Angle

The study varies the smaller planet rotors’ oblique angle relative to the central sun turbine among 0, 30, 60, and 90 degrees. (source: sources/cj1.md)

At the reported 5-diameter center-to-center condition and 6 m/s, 30 degrees gives the highest central `Cp`, `0.3405`, at TSR `1.5`. The 0-, 60-, and 90-degree cases give `Cp` values of `0.2916`, `0.3088`, and `0.2710`, respectively. (source: sources/cj1.md)

The source attributes the 30-degree result to the two upstream planet turbines increasing and stabilizing pressure around the central blades. (source: sources/cj1.md)

> Uncertainty: the reported result concerns only the central rotor in a 2D simulation. The 30-degree case is lower than the isolated rotor at TSR 1.0 and 1.25, so it is not a uniform performance improvement. (source: sources/cj1.md)
