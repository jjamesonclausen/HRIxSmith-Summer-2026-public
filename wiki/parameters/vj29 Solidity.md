## Solidity

This `vj29` review includes solidity as one of the central variables repeatedly used to analyze H-type Darrieus performance. (source: sources/vj29.md)

- The abstract lists solidity among the key design elements reviewed for H-type Darrieus rotors. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- In the factors section, the review says pitch angle and solidity were both found to affect the aerodynamic performance of self-starting VAWTs to a high extent in one cited numerical study. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- It also cites profile comparisons where reported gains or losses depend on the solidity range, reinforcing that airfoil choice and solidity interact rather than behaving as independent knobs. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- The review therefore treats solidity as a core design variable that must be tuned together with blade profile, TSR, and blade number rather than optimized in isolation. (source: sources/vj29.md)
