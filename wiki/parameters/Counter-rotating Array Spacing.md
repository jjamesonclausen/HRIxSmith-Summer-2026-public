## Counter-rotating Array Spacing

The source treats close spacing and counter-rotation as array design parameters for improving VAWT wind-farm power density. (source: sources/va3.md)

## Parameter

- The shown array uses VAWTs 10 m tall and 1.2 m in diameter. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Turbine spacing is 1.6 turbine diameters. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Adjacent turbines counter-rotate so airflow induced between turbines is oriented in the same direction. (source: sources/va3.md)
- The source says this reduces horizontal wind shear or velocity-gradient turbulence relative to adjacent turbines rotating in the same direction. (source: sources/va3.md)

![Figure 38. Array of counter-rotating vertical-axis wind turbines 10 m tall and 1.2 m in diameter relative to a person 1.9 m in height. Turbine spacing is 1.6 turbine diameters.](../../images/va3-fig38.jpg)

## Outcome

- The source says close-spaced counter-rotating VAWTs can slightly improve performance relative to isolated turbines, averaged across wind directions. (source: sources/va3.md)
- It contrasts this with 20-50 percent performance reductions for HAWTs at similar turbine spacing. (source: sources/va3.md)
- It reports an approximate wind-farm power flux of 18 W/m2, described as 6-9 times the power density of modern HAWT wind farms. (source: sources/va3.md)
- It says the approach is most effective for VAWTs operating at TSR greater than 2. (source: sources/va3.md)
