## Gearbox Sizing

This `vj23` paper treats gearbox sizing as a core power-transfer choice in the small Darrieus design. (source: sources/vj23.md)

## Parameter

- The source says the gearbox or transmission moves and changes the rotating motor power. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It states that a larger turbine-side gear and smaller generator-side gear are needed to obtain large turbine torque at the generator. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- In the results discussion, it mentions gearbox use around `1:60`. (source: sources/vj23.md)

## Outcome

- The paper says that if the turbine-side gear is larger than the generator rotor part, the torque at the rotor is greater and the output is greater. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It presents the gearbox as the mechanism that turns low wheel rotation into higher generator rotation. (source: sources/vj23.md)

> Uncertainty: the source gives only a qualitative gearbox recommendation and one approximate ratio mention, not a validated quantitative optimization or measured before/after comparison. (source: sources/vj23.md)
