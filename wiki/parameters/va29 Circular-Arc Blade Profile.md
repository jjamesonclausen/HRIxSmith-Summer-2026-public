## va29 Circular-Arc Blade Profile

The source parameterized Savonius blades as circular arcs, optimizing endpoints and curvature parameters while constraining the outer rotor radius and avoiding blade intersections. (source: sources/va29.md)

- The two-blade optimum was flatter than the classical semicircular `s/d = 0.1` rotor, had practically zero overlap, and raised cP from `0.217` to `0.242` in the source's 3D CFD model. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The optimized four- and six-blade designs resemble scooplet-based configurations and reached cP `0.318` and `0.321`, respectively, at `AR = 1.5`. (source: sources/va29.md)
- The four-blade shape improved cP 3% relative to the preceding 2D-CFD-optimized geometry when both were compared with matched 3D CFD and aspect ratio. (source: sources/va29.md)
