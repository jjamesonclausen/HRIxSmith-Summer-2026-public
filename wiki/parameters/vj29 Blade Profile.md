## Blade Profile

This `vj29` review treats blade profile as one of the main design levers governing H-type Darrieus aerodynamic performance. (source: sources/vj29.md)

- The review says optimized airfoils can improve `Cp` by about `15%-20%` over conventional `NACA 00xx` sections. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- It highlights `S1046` as a commonly strong symmetric airfoil for straight-bladed H-rotors, with reported `Cp,max` around `0.35` or slightly above in open-rotor cases. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- It says cambered sections such as `S815`, `EN0005`, `NREL S823`, and `NACA 6712` tend to improve self-starting and low-TSR torque, but can suffer higher drag penalties at higher TSR. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- The review reports `NREL S823` as one cited example with about `6%` higher `Cp,max` and higher static torque than `NACA 0018` over a range of solidities. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- It also says thicker sections such as `NACA 0021` can improve self-starting at low wind speeds compared with thinner sections like `NACA 0012`, even though that can reduce maximum `Cp` at higher Reynolds number and TSR. (source: sources/vj29.md)
- Low-TSR profile modifications are also emphasized: slotted `NACA0018` / slotted flap cases are reported to increase low-TSR `Cp` by about `19%-27%`, and blunt trailing-edge designs by about `33%`. (source: sources/vj29.md)
