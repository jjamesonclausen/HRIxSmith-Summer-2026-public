## Blade Material

This `vj7` review studies blade material choice for straight-bladed VAWTs, with emphasis on fatigue life, stiffness, density, cost, and manufacturability. (source: sources/vj7.md)

## Parameter Change

- The source compares commonly used aluminum blades with alternative materials including wood/epoxy and fiberglass-reinforced plastic (FRP). (source: sources/vj7.md)
- The detailed design comparison in the review focuses especially on aluminum versus pultruded FRP. (source: sources/vj7.md)
- The review frames the material choice around long design life, very high fatigue-cycle demand, stiffness, density, cost, and manufacturability rather than around a single aerodynamic metric. (source: sources/vj7.md)

## Outcome

- The review concludes that pultruded FRP is a prospective alternative and is superior to conventionally used aluminum in the design analyses reported. (source: sources/vj7.md)
- Aluminum is described as common but limited by poor fatigue properties under cyclic loading, which is a central issue for VAWT blades expected to see more than `10^8` fatigue cycles over a `20-30 year` lifetime. (source: sources/vj7.md)
- Wood/epoxy and FRP are discussed as lower-density alternatives, but FRP is the source's preferred structural option because it combines low mass with high stiffness and better fatigue resistance. (source: sources/vj7.md)
- The source is mostly a structural-material review, so the reported benefit is stronger expected blade durability and design suitability rather than a clean rotor `Cp` gain. (source: sources/vj7.md)
