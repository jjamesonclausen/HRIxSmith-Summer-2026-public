## Capacity Factor

The fraction of a turbine's theoretical maximum output that is actually generated over a period of time. (source: sources/vj14.md)

- The vj14 paper defines capacity factor as actual electricity generated divided by the theoretical maximum for the turbine's nominal capacity and operating time. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- For the 5 kW turbine in the paper, 100% operation for a year would equal 43,800 kWh. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- The measured site capacity factor was 0.50% over three years, while the paper uses 9% and 20.5% as comparison cases from the literature. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- The study finds that small changes in capacity factor can strongly change the LCA outcome. (source: sources/vj14.md)
