## Savonius Wind Turbine Cluster

A cluster of multiple Savonius turbines arranged so wake interaction and wind-direction effects can change the total output. (source: sources/vj13.md)

- The vj13 paper studies a three-turbine cluster made from a conventional two-blade Savonius rotor with overlap ratio `0.1`, `D = 0.95 m`, and `H = 1 m`. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- Proper arrangement can produce positive coupling, but a layout optimized for one direction can lose performance when the wind shifts. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- In the study, cluster performance depended much more on wind direction than on wind speed. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The cluster performed better than an isolated turbine at all three sites when the installation orientation matched the local wind rose. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- Reported annual output ratios were about `1.25`, `1.27`, and `1.16/1.17` versus the isolated turbine. (source: sources/vj13.md)
