## Helical Darrieus

A Darrieus rotor whose blades are twisted helically around the shaft. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Named in the report as one of the three main Darrieus types. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- The helical layout is used to smooth torque and spread blade loading over the rotation. (source: sources/va4.md, sources/va7.md)
