## Helical Darrieus

A Darrieus rotor whose blades are twisted helically around the shaft. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - The blades are swept helically around the rotor rather than lying in one plane. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
  - The helical angle spreads blade loading through the rotation. (source: sources/va7.md)
- Performance:
  - The helical layout reduces shaft ripple and lowers moment-coefficient standard deviation as helix angle increases. (source: sources/va7.md)
  - A 60-degree helical blade performed best among the tested helix angles in the cited study. (source: sources/va7.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - Higher helix angles smooth loading but can reduce peak power performance. (source: sources/va7.md)
  - The geometry is more complex than straight-bladed layouts. > Inference. (source: sources/va7.md)

- Named in the report as one of the three main Darrieus types. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- The helical layout is used to smooth torque and spread blade loading over the rotation. (source: sources/va4.md, sources/va7.md)
