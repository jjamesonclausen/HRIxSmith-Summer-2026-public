## Troposkien Darrieus

A curved-blade Darrieus design shaped to follow the troposkien form. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Named in the report as a Darrieus rotor family distinct from H-rotor and eggbeater layouts. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- The curved blade shape is associated with large turbines and structural/aerodynamic tradeoffs near the top and bottom of the rotor. (source: sources/vj4.md)
