## Darrieus above Savonius

A hybrid configuration where the Savonius rotor sits below the Darrieus rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Geometry:
  - The Savonius is placed beneath the Darrieus, changing the vertical stacking of the hybrid. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Performance:
  - The report lists it as one of several hybrid arrangement variants explored in the concept space. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Tradeoffs:
  - Different vertical placement changes the interaction between startup torque and lift-based operation. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Listed by the report as one of the documented hybrid configurations in the design search. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- It is one of the many ways the report varies rotor placement to balance startup and efficiency. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
