## D-rotor / Eggbeater Darrieus

A curved-blade Darrieus variant often called the eggbeater rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)

- Named in the report as one of the three main Darrieus types. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Curved blades give the rotor a varying angle of attack through the cycle. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- The curved form is associated with large turbines and strong aerodynamic loading near the top and bottom of the rotor. (source: sources/vj4.md)
