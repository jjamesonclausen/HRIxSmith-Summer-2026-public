## Helical Hybrid

A hybrid VAWT combining helical Darrieus blades with an internal helical Savonius rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md, sources/vj8.md)

- Selected by the HRI report after concept screening and prototype testing. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- Used asymmetrical helical Darrieus blades surrounding a widened internal helical Savonius rotor. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
- The report uses this as the final design direction for its phase-1 prototype. (source: sources/HRI2526.md)
