## Turbine Concept Selection

This page helps choose the first turbine family before detailed design starts.

- Choose [[VAWT]] when wind direction changes often or yaw complexity is undesirable. (source: sources/vj1.md, sources/HRI2526.md)
- Choose [[Savonius Turbine]] when self-starting and low-speed torque matter more than peak efficiency. (source: sources/vj2.md, sources/n2.md)
- Choose [[Darrieus Turbine]] or [[H-VAWT]] when lift-based efficiency is the priority. (source: sources/vj1.md, sources/vj4.md)
- Choose [[Hybrid VAWT]] when you want to combine self-starting behavior with lift-driven efficiency. (source: sources/vj2.md)
- Choose [[Helical VAWT]] when smoother torque and lower load pulsation matter. (source: sources/vj4.md, sources/va7.md)
- Choose [[va5 J-Type VAWT|J-Type VAWT]] when a compact rooftop or small-scale deployment is the target. (source: sources/va5.md)
- Choose a contra-rotating concept when stability and wind-energy recovery are the main goals. (source: sources/vj8.md)
