## Wind Deflector

Passive flow-augmentation device placed around or upstream of a VAWT to redirect wind toward the advancing blade and away from the returning blade. (source: sources/vj27.md)

- The review says the main purpose is to reduce negative torque on the returning blade while increasing positive torque on the forwarding blade. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- It groups wind deflectors into flat-plate, airfoil-shaped, and compound structures such as V-shapes or kite-shaped twin-turbine layouts. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- It says the main design variables are shape, orientation angle, upstream/downstream position, distance from the rotor, and deflector length or height relative to rotor dimensions. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- For small urban VAWTs, the review treats deflectors as one of the most effective passive augmentation techniques because they can raise `Cp` materially without the moving-part complexity of active pitch systems. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- It also warns that wrong deflector orientation can hurt performance enough to make the turbine perform worse than the no-deflector case. (source: sources/vj27.md)
- The review says experimental evidence is still much thinner than CFD evidence, so deflector concepts should not be treated as fully mature only because simulations look strong. (source: sources/vj27.md)
