## QBlade

Simulation software used in `vj22` to analyze low-Re airfoil behavior and then test a small straight-bladed VAWT rotor design. (source: sources/vj22.md)

- The paper uses QBlade to compare `NACA 0012` and `NACA 0015` over Reynolds numbers from `50000` to `1000000`. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- It evaluates `Cl`, `Cd`, and `Cl/Cd` against angle of attack to identify favorable operating regions. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- The source then uses the airfoil data in a rotor simulation to compare `Cp` versus `TSR` and power versus wind speed for different blade counts. (source: sources/vj22.md)
- In this paper, QBlade is used as a comparative low-wind design-screening tool rather than as an experimentally validated final-performance method. (source: sources/vj22.md)
