## Bin Method

Standard approach for processing wind speed data for turbine performance analysis. (source: sources/va1.md)

- Wind data is grouped into discrete intervals (“bins”), typically 1 m/s wide. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Frequency or probability of wind speeds is calculated for each bin. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Based on IEC 61400-12 standard for wind turbine performance testing. (source: sources/va1.md)

- Enables consistent comparison between turbines using the same wind dataset. (source: sources/va1.md)

> Limitation: Does not capture directional effects or fine-grained turbulence. (source: sources/va1.md)
