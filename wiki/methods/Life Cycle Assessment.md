## Life Cycle Assessment

Cradle-to-grave environmental accounting method used to evaluate the impacts of a turbine and its support infrastructure. (source: sources/vj14.md)

- The source follows ISO 14040 framing and uses a functional unit of 1 kWh of electricity generated. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- It models raw material extraction, manufacturing, transport, installation, operation, and end-of-life treatment. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- The paper compares a do-nothing end-of-life case with a recycling-plus-incineration case. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- LCIA is performed with the CML2001 Jan. 2016 method in GaBi, covering 11 impact categories. (source: sources/vj14.md)
- The method is used to compare actual site performance against hypothetical capacity-factor cases. (source: sources/vj14.md)
