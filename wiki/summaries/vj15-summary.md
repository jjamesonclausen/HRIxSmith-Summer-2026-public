## vj15 Summary

This paper tests harmonic variable-pitch functions for an H-type Darrieus turbine and finds that Case 1 with a 3-degree amplitude performs best overall. (source: sources/vj15.md)

- The best setting improves power by about 34 percent relative to fixed pitch. (source: sources/vj15.md)
- Variable pitch also improves self-starting, especially around the azimuth range where the fixed rotor is weakest. (source: sources/vj15.md)
- Larger amplitudes, especially 20 degrees, hurt performance instead of helping it. (source: sources/vj15.md)
- The paper attributes the gain to lower effective angle of attack, weaker dynamic stall, and reduced wake loss. (source: sources/vj15.md)
