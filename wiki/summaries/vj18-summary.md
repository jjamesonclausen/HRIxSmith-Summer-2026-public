## vj18 Summary

This review maps variable-design approaches for VAWTs and groups them by how they change the rotor during operation. (source: sources/vj18.md)

- Variable pitch is the most developed family in the review and is presented as a path to better lift, torque, self-starting, and storm protection. (source: sources/vj18.md)
- Adaptive flaps, Gurney flaps, morphing blades, synthetic jets, movable mass blocks, and variable swept area are all reviewed as ways to improve performance or reduce loads. (source: sources/vj18.md)
- The paper keeps returning to the same tradeoff: more adaptability usually means more mechanical complexity, actuation cost, and durability risk. (source: sources/vj18.md)
- The review favors simpler passive systems and more field validation before commercialization claims are trusted. (source: sources/vj18.md)
