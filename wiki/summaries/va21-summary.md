## va21 Summary

Experimental and CFD study of a small rooftop VAWT installed on a Kolkata building, focused on the prototype's mechanical layout, electrical output, vibration behavior, and numerical validation. (source: sources/va21.md)

Key points:
- The prototype is a four-bladed rooftop VAWT installed on the south-west corner of a building roof in Kolkata, mounted on a parapet-supported brick pillar about `21 m` above ground. (source: sources/va21.md)
- The paper reports rotor speed increasing from `75 rpm` to `100 rpm` as rooftop wind magnitude increased, and separate power-generation tests over `55-115 rpm` produced `3-11.9 V`, `20-130 mA`, and `0.05-2.7 W`. (source: sources/va21.md)
- The vibration measurements show generally increasing but irregular displacement, velocity, and acceleration with shaft speed, while peak frequency remains highly random. (source: sources/va21.md)
- The CFD model uses ANSYS Fluent `16.2` with a `k-epsilon` turbulence model and reports less than `10%` deviation in rotor speed and less than `20%` deviation in voltage and power relative to measured data. (source: sources/va21.md)
- The commercial section reports about `INR 75k` installed cost and an estimated `7-15 year` payback, but the same source also describes the prototype output as supplementary rather than a full replacement for home power supply. (source: sources/va21.md)
