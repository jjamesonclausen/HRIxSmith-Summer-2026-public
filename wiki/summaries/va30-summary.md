## va30 Summary

This wind-tunnel study tested 15 two- and three-bucket Savonius configurations, making it a direct experimental benchmark for CFD validation. (source: sources/va30.md)

- The tests used semicircular two-bucket rotors with bucket radius `0.25 m`, `180 degrees` of arc, bucket heights `1` or `1.5 m`, and gap ratios from `S/D = 0` to `0.20`. (source: sources/va30.md)
- Tests at nominal `7` and `14 m/s` measured static torque every `10 degrees`, then dynamic torque and rotational speed across the operating range after steady state was reached. (source: sources/va30.md)
- The authors corrected all reported coefficients for friction tare torque and wind-tunnel blockage. (source: sources/va30.md)
- For the tested configurations, two buckets gave higher peak cP than three, while three buckets reduced the angular variation of static torque. (source: sources/va30.md)
- The reported preferred gap range is `S/D = 0.10-0.15`; the paper recommends two two-bucket stages offset by `90 degrees` to improve minimum starting torque. (source: sources/va30.md)
