## n2 Source Summary

Summary of `sources/n2.md`. (source: sources/n2.md)

Condensed notes from VAWT project including parameters, design insights, prototype selection, and recommendations.

Key points:
- Reiterates core performance parameters (cut-in, TSR, CP, swept area, capacity factor). (source: sources/n2.md)
- Confirms Darrieus vs Savonius tradeoffs and introduces detailed Savonius optimization parameters. (source: sources/n2.md)
- Final selected design: helical hybrid (outer Darrieus + inner Savonius). (source: sources/n2.md)
- Economic result: extremely poor ROI at low wind speeds (~5000 year break-even). (source: sources/n2.md)

Additional insights:
- Inefficiencies arise from wake interaction, dynamic stall, and negative torque. (source: sources/n2.md)
- Higher wind environments (e.g., skyscrapers, bridges) significantly improve feasibility. (source: sources/n2.md)
- CFD recommended as primary future optimization pathway. (source: sources/n2.md)
