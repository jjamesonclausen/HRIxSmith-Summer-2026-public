## n1 Summary

Overview of vertical-axis wind turbines (VAWTs), including comparisons with horizontal-axis turbines (HAWTs), aerodynamic principles, design parameters, and optimization opportunities. (source: sources/n1.md)

- VAWTs are omnidirectional and suited for turbulent, urban environments but are generally less efficient than HAWTs. (source: sources/n1.md)
- Key turbine parameters include cut-in speed, rated speed, cut-out speed, and starting torque. (source: sources/n1.md)
- Two main VAWT types:
  - Darrieus (lift-based): higher efficiency but poor self-starting. (source: sources/n1.md)
  - Savonius (drag-based): self-starting but low efficiency. (source: sources/n1.md)
- Hybrid designs combine both to balance efficiency and self-starting. (source: sources/n1.md)
- CFD is used to simulate turbine performance and requires careful setup and mesh quality. (source: sources/n1.md)
- Urban airflow is turbulent and complex, affecting turbine performance. (source: sources/n1.md)
