## va6 Source Summary

Summary of `sources/va6.md`. (source: sources/va6.md)

- The paper compares HAWTs and VAWTs across efficiency, torque, noise, maintenance, capacity, and siting. (source: sources/va6.md)
- It concludes HAWTs are more efficient, but VAWTs are quieter and better for compact or urban settings. (source: sources/va6.md)
- The paper gives example values for power coefficient, tip-speed ratio, and peak efficiency across several turbine types. (source: sources/va6.md)
