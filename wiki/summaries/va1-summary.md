## va1 Summary

Comparison of lift-driven and drag-driven VAWTs for low-wind urban environments, focusing on Annual Energy Output (AEO). (source: sources/va1.md)

- Lift-driven turbines have higher efficiency and dominate energy production at higher wind speeds. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Drag-driven turbines perform better at low wind speeds and can self-start easily. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Urban wind speeds are typically low (~3.2 m/s), making turbine selection context-dependent. (source: sources/va1.md)
- Despite different performance characteristics, total AEO can be similar between designs. (source: sources/va1.md)
- CFD and better site-specific wind data are important for accurate predictions. (source: sources/va1.md)
