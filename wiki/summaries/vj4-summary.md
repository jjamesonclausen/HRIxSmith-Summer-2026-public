## vj4 Source Summary

Summary of `sources/vj4.md`. (source: sources/vj4.md)

This paper compares small VAWT blade architectures using a BE-M load predictor and experimental validation. (source: sources/vj4.md)

Key points:
- Compares two- vs three-bladed rotors, symmetrical vs cambered airfoils, and straight vs helical blade layouts. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- Validates the BE-M predictor against Sandia Darrieus data and tangential force measurements. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- Uses fixed swept area and solidity to isolate the effect of architecture choices. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- Finds three-bladed rotors perform better than two-bladed rotors in this setup. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- Finds the helical rotor reduces pulsating torque and thrust and broadens the high-performance TSR range. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- Finds the DU 06-W-200 cambered airfoil helps low-TSR start-up but is weaker in medium-low wind exploitation. (source: sources/vj4.md)

Findings:
- The helical configuration is the best architecture overall for smooth loads and wind-energy exploitation. (source: sources/vj4.md)
- The paper still notes higher manufacturing cost and the need for more experimental tests on Gorlov-type rotors. (source: sources/vj4.md)
