## vj23 Summary

This paper is a simple design-oriented discussion of a small Darrieus-type VAWT for low-wind Indonesian conditions, emphasizing low mounting height, blade count, and gearbox use. (source: sources/vj23.md)

- The source argues that Darrieus VAWTs suit changing wind direction because they do not need yawing and can place the generator and gearbox near the ground for easier maintenance. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It presents one 3-bladed Darrieus concept with a gearbox-chain drive to a generator. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- The abstract reports that with `3` blades the turbine gets torque above `40 rpm` at wind speeds of `4.5 to 5 m/s`. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- The paper says a larger turbine-side gearbox and smaller generator-side gear are needed to increase generator torque and mentions a typical gearbox use around `1:60`. (source: sources/vj23.md)
- It also argues that more blades can capture wind more effectively, but the paper's own text says increasing blade count can create more blocking and reduce performance. (source: sources/vj23.md)

> Discrepancy: the abstract highlights a `3`-blade case, while the later discussion says a `4`-blade turbine has greater torque and a more stable torque profile. Treat the blade-count conclusion as source-internal and not cleanly resolved. (source: sources/vj23.md)
