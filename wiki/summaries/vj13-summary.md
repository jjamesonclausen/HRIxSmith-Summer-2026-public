## vj13 Summary

This paper studies a three-turbine Savonius cluster in real wind roses and shows that wind direction and wake interaction matter more than wind speed alone. (source: sources/vj13.md)

- The source uses 2-D transient CFD in Fluent with `k-ω SST`, a variable rotational speed control strategy, and validation against wind-tunnel data. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The cluster is built from a conventional two-blade Savonius turbine with overlap ratio `0.1`, `D = 0.95 m`, and `H = 1 m`. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- Wind direction-induced wake effects are the main cause of power loss, and some orientations can push a turbine's `F` below `0.1`, forcing the rotational speed to zero. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The best installation orientation depends on site-specific wind roses, not just prevailing direction or frequency. (source: sources/vj13.md)
- The paper reports annual power output ratios of about `1.25`, `1.27`, and `1.16/1.17` relative to the isolated turbine at the three test sites. (source: sources/vj13.md)

> Unverified: the paper reports Site 3 as `1.16` in Section 4.3 and `1.17` in the abstract/conclusion. (source: sources/vj13.md)
