## vj2 Source Summary

Summary of `sources/vj2.md`. (source: sources/vj2.md)

This paper studies a Savonius-Darrieus hybrid wind turbine using CFD.

Key points:
- Starts with a helical Savonius rotor placed inside a three-bladed helical Darrieus rotor. (source: sources/vj2.md)
- Tests torque at nine attack angles from 0 to 120 degrees. (source: sources/vj2.md)
- Removes the shaft from the Savonius rotor as one optimization. (source: sources/vj2.md)
- Tests a second configuration with the Savonius rotor split into two halves and moved outside the Darrieus rotor. (source: sources/vj2.md)
- Reports average torque increases of 10.5% and 22.3% for the two optimized configurations. (source: sources/vj2.md)

Method and result notes:
- Uses SolidWorks Flow Simulation on a 3D domain with mesh refinement. (source: sources/vj2.md)
- Concludes that both shaft removal and moving the Savonius rotor outside the Darrieus rotor improve torque. (source: sources/vj2.md)
