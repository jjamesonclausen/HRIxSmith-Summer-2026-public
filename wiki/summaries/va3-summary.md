## va3 Summary

Overview article on vertical-axis wind turbines, covering drag-based, lift-based, and helical variants, plus array effects and practical deployment tradeoffs. (source: sources/va3.md)

- Defines tip speed ratio and uses it to distinguish primarily drag-based designs from lift-based designs. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Describes the Savonius concept as a simple impulse rotor and the Darrieus concept as an aerodynamic lift-based rotor. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Notes the H-Rotor / H-type family as a later VAWT form and discusses helical wind turbine variants with ground-level generators. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Emphasizes VAWT advantages in omnidirectional wind capture, lower noise, simpler maintenance, and potential use in urban or constrained sites. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Argues that large HAWTs face scaling, logistics, and tower-stability limits, while VAWTs can be built lower and tolerate harsher environments. (source: sources/va3.md)
- Discusses turbine arrays, including wake interactions and the possibility that downstream VAWTs can benefit from correctly positioned upstream machines. (source: sources/va3.md)
- One Turby power-curve figure did not recover as a clean caption/image pair in the export and is marked unverified in the source markdown. (source: sources/va3.md)
