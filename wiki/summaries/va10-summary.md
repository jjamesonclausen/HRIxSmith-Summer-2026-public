## va10 Summary

Review paper on CFD simulation techniques for Darrieus VAWTs, focused on reliable setup choices, turbulence-model selection, and the use of CFD to study geometry, wake interaction, aeroacoustics, skewed wind, guide vanes, and self-starting. (source: sources/va10.md)

![Source figure](../../images/va10-fig7.jpg)

Key points:
- Frames VAWTs as attractive for urban and turbulent wind because of yaw-free operation, ground-level machinery access, lower noise, and tolerance of changing inflow direction. (source: sources/va10.md)
- Recommends grid-sensitivity and domain-sensitivity studies, blade and wake refinement, wall-resolution checks, convergence checks, and careful time stepping for reliable CFD. (source: sources/va10.md)
- Reviews `k-epsilon`, `k-omega SST`, transition SST, LES, and hybrid RANS-LES approaches, with higher-fidelity models used when separation and unsteady wake detail matter more. (source: sources/va10.md)
- Reviews how solidity, blade number, airfoil shape, guide vanes, wake interaction, skewed wind, aeroacoustics, and self-starting affect Darrieus VAWT performance in CFD studies. (source: sources/va10.md)
