## vj9 Source Summary

This paper studies CFD-based optimization of Savonius wind turbine blade shapes, with a novel scooplet-based design that outperforms the classical and modified classical Savonius cases. (source: sources/vj9.md)

- The paper compares several prior Savonius modifications, then focuses on a simple constant-thickness, circular-arc-based scooplet design that is easier to manufacture. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The modeling uses 2D CFD for optimization and 3D CFD for validation, with the SST two-equation turbulence model. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The paper reports that the scooplet-based design achieves a 39% improvement in maximum power coefficient over the classical Savonius design. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The modified classical Savonius design improves the classical case by about 4.5%, but the scooplet-based design performs better overall. (source: sources/vj9.md)
- The 3D CFD validation shows the optimized designs peak near the design TSR of 0.9, and the scooplet-based design remains the strongest performer among the tested shapes. (source: sources/vj9.md)
